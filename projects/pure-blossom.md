# Pure Blossom — Klaviyo Lifecycle Marketing Client Project

**Project type:** Real client project  
**Platform:** Klaviyo  
**Brand:** Pure Blossom — eCommerce brand

## Project Overview

Pure Blossom was a client project focused on building and implementing a structured lifecycle email marketing system in Klaviyo.

The work covered the customer journey from newsletter signup through purchase, repeat engagement, and reactivation. I focused on creating practical automations that matched customer behavior and the brand's communication style.

## Lifecycle Flows

### Welcome Flow

**Trigger:** New subscriber joins the newsletter list.

**Sequence:**
1. Welcome / brand introduction
2. Product and value-focused email
3. Offer / purchase incentive

**Purpose:** Introduce the brand, build initial awareness, and encourage the first purchase.

---

### Abandoned Checkout Flow

**Trigger:** Customer starts checkout but does not complete the purchase.

**Sequence:**
1. Delay
2. Reminder email
3. Follow-up email

**Purpose:** Recover potential lost purchases by reminding customers about the products they were considering.

---

### Abandoned Cart Flow

**Trigger:** Customer adds a product to cart but does not complete the transaction.

**Sequence:**
1. Delay
2. Cart reminder email
3. Follow-up reminder

**Purpose:** Re-engage shoppers who showed purchase intent but did not complete the transaction.

---

### Winback Flow

**Trigger:** Customer reaches the defined post-purchase inactivity period.

**Sequence:**
1. Re-engagement message
2. Follow-up communication

**Purpose:** Encourage previous customers to return and make another purchase.

---

### Sunset Flow

**Trigger:** Subscriber enters the sunset audience based on engagement/inactivity criteria.

**Logic:**
- Identify whether the profile has placed an order
- Send a re-engagement sequence
- Provide an opportunity to return
- Move persistently unengaged profiles toward suppression

**Purpose:** Protect list quality while giving inactive subscribers a final opportunity to re-engage.
