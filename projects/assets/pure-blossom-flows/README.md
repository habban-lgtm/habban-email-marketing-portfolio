# Pure Blossom — Klaviyo Lifecycle Flow Visual Evidence

This section presents selected screenshots from the Pure Blossom Klaviyo client project to demonstrate the lifecycle flow architecture, automation logic, and customer journey strategy.

> **Client confidentiality:** Performance results, revenue, conversion data, and customer-level information are intentionally not shown in these screenshots. The visuals are provided to demonstrate the flow structure and implementation work while protecting client privacy.

---

## 1. Welcome Flow — New Subscriber Journey

Designed to introduce new subscribers to Pure Blossom, build initial brand awareness, communicate product value, and encourage the first purchase.

**Journey structure:** Subscribe → Welcome Email → Product/Value Email → Promotional Email

**Primary objectives:**
- Introduce the Pure Blossom brand
- Build initial subscriber engagement
- Communicate product value
- Encourage the first purchase

**Screenshot:**  
![Welcome Flow](welcome-flow.png)

---

## 2. Abandoned Checkout Flow — Purchase Recovery

Built to re-engage customers who started the checkout process but did not complete their purchase.

**Journey structure:** Checkout Started → Delay → Recovery Email → Follow-up Email

**Primary objectives:**
- Recover potentially lost purchases
- Remind customers about an incomplete checkout
- Create a timely purchase recovery journey

**Screenshot:**  
![Abandoned Checkout Flow](abandoned-checkout-flow.png)

---

## 3. Abandoned Cart Flow — Cart Recovery

Designed to re-engage shoppers who added products to their cart but did not complete the purchase process.

**Journey structure:** Added to Cart → Delay → Cart Reminder → Follow-up Email

**Primary objectives:**
- Re-engage high-intent shoppers
- Remind customers about products left in their cart
- Encourage customers to return and complete their purchase

**Screenshot:**  
![Abandoned Cart Flow](abandoned-cart-flow.png)

---

## 4. Winback Flow — Customer Reactivation

Built to re-engage previous customers after a defined period of inactivity and encourage them to return to the brand.

**Journey structure:** Previous Purchase → Inactivity Period → Re-engagement Email → Follow-up

**Primary objectives:**
- Reactivate inactive customers
- Encourage repeat purchases
- Support long-term customer retention

**Screenshot:**  
![Winback Flow](winback-flow.png)

---

## 5. Sunset Flow — Re-engagement & List Hygiene

Designed to identify and re-engage inactive subscribers before moving persistently unengaged profiles toward suppression.

**Journey structure:** Newsletter Audience → Engagement Split → Re-engagement → Final Reminder → Unengaged Profile Handling

**Primary objectives:**
- Give inactive subscribers an opportunity to re-engage
- Reduce unnecessary communication with inactive profiles
- Support healthier audience engagement and list hygiene

**Screenshot:**  
![Sunset Flow](sunset-flow.png)
